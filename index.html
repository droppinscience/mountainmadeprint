FAQ

Good questions

Is there a minimum order?

Small runs are welcome. Because there are no screens to set up, a dozen glasses or a handful of shirts is no problem. Larger orders get better per-piece pricing.

What file should I send?

A PNG with a transparent background, an SVG, or a PDF works best. If all you have is a photo of your sign or a business card, send it anyway and we'll talk about cleaning it up.

How long does it take?

Turnaround is 3 to 5 business days after you approve your proof. Need it sooner? Ask, and we'll tell you honestly if we can make it.

Are the glasses dishwasher safe?

Hand-washing is recommended for printed glassware and tumblers so the print lasts. Don't soak them or scrub the print.

Can you print one-off gifts?

Yes. Custom glasses, tumblers and shirts make good birthday, wedding party and groomsmen gifts.
